MEETING RHYTHM PROFILES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purity Ratio: Fraction of bins dominated by one coordination type (score 1 or 2)
  High purity → Clear phase separation (focused phases)
  Low purity → Mixed coordination throughout

Mixed Ratio: Fraction of bins with balanced score 1 and score 2
  High mixed → Simultaneous coordination and decision-making
  Low mixed → Teams switch between modes cleanly

n_Transitions: Number of large entropy jumps (>0.3)
  High transitions → Teams deliberately shift between phases
  Low transitions → Steady state coordination style

Entropy Trend: Change from first third to last third
  Positive → Teams transition toward more mixed coordination
  Negative → Teams focus/narrow coordination over time

PURPOSE
================================================================================
Shift from aggregate entropy metrics (P0-P1) to understanding HOW Coordination
and Decision Practices (CDP) are used across different dimensions:
  - Content: What are score 1 vs score 2 utterances?
  - Speaker: Who drives advanced coordination? How balanced is participation?
  - Timing: When do teams shift between basic and advanced practices?
  - Cohort: Do patterns differ across conference years?
  - Roles: Do specific speaker roles drive CDP adoption?
